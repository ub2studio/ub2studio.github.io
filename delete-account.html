Delete Your UB2 Finance Account

UB2 Finance lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data directly within the app — no need to contact support or fill out a form.

How to delete your account

  1. Open UB2 Finance and go to Settings
  2. Scroll to the Security section and tap "Delete Account"
  3. Confirm you understand this action is permanent
  4. Confirm a second time — this final step permanently deletes everything

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes all of the following data, immediately and in full:

  • All transactions and expense history
  • Net worth entries and history
  • Bills and subscriptions
  • Goals and their progress
  • Debts and payoff tracking
  • Budget settings
  • Money Timeline events
  • Your account credentials and sign-in information
Retention: UB2 Finance does not retain any of your data after account deletion. There is no grace period or backup copy kept on our servers — deletion is immediate and permanent.

Can't access the app?

If you're unable to open the app to complete these steps, email us at contact@ub2studio.com from the email address associated with your account, and we'll process the deletion manually within 7 days.
